The Happyish
Bill of Rights

✳happy-ish
  1. 01

    You have the right to go at your own pace.

  2. 02

    You have the right to say “I don’t know.”

  3. 03

    You have the right not to be perfect (whatever that is).

  4. 04

    You have the right to hyperfocus.

  5. 05

    You have the right to stop.

  6. 06

    You have the right to make your own choices and decisions.

  7. 07

    You have the right to ask for help, or not.

  8. 08

    You have the right to all of your feelings.

  9. 09

    You have the right to change your mind at any time.

  10. 10

    You have the right to go at your own pace and ask for things to be explained differently.

  11. 11

    You have the right to rest without earning it.

  12. 12

    You have the right to choose your own priorities and goals.

  13. 13

    You have the right to dream, explore new possibilities, have hope and change direction.

  14. 14

    You have the right to enjoy, play, have fun and be happy-ish.

In our sessions

Your rights, in practice.

Ask for a break, a shorter step or another explanation. You can decline AI or screen sharing, change your goal, or stop a session. You do not need to justify those choices.

If you return after a gap, we agree what matters to you now. Any future optional AI memory would need to be visible, correctable and removable by you; it would not hold you to an old goal.

If an adjustment is not working, or you have a concern about the support, contact Karl by email or telephone. Tell us how you would prefer to discuss it. Contact details are available without opening any of the cards above.
