Keep the first message simple.

Your name, a way to reply and a short description of what you need help with are enough. Please don’t send passwords, bank details or sensitive health information.

What happens next?

We’ll use your message to respond and work out a sensible next step with you. There is no automatic assessment or AI intake process. We agree session times directly; we do not offer instant appointments or an urgent response service. If you need a particular contact format or adjustment, tell us in your message.

When you need urgent or specialist help

Happyish is not an emergency service and does not replace medical, legal, financial or other regulated professional advice. For an immediate emergency in the UK, call 999. Where specialist support is needed, we can discuss an appropriate next step and help you prepare for that conversation. If you have a concern about Happyish’s support or an accessibility barrier, use the contact details above. This is not a confidential crisis or safeguarding reporting service. For urgent mental-health support, see our urgent support page.
