A small beginning

Happyish is developing its initial one-to-one offer. One possible approach is six weekly sessions of around 60–90 minutes. Timing, format and availability will be discussed rather than assumed.

  1. 01

    Start with a conversation

    Tell us what you’re trying to achieve. You don’t need a perfectly formed plan.

  2. 02

    Choose a few practical goals

    Agree what would make everyday life easier, and what progress would look like for you.

  3. 03

    Work on real tasks together

    We explain, demonstrate and practise at your pace. No judgement, no rushing.

  4. 04

    Take more of the lead

    First we show you. Then we do it together. Then you do it with support. Eventually, you do it yourself.

  5. 05

    Review and find a good stopping point

    Look at what you can now do independently. Finish, move to lighter-touch support or consider another short block if useful.
