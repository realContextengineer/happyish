Safety, choice and respect

Safeguarding
and support.

Happyish is a UK-based service offering informal, trauma-informed digital mentoring. Safeguarding is part of how we work from the start. Your safety, choices and boundaries matter throughout.

What mentoring is — and isn’t

Mentoring is practical, person-led support with digital tasks and confidence. It is not psychotherapy, counselling, a crisis response or a substitute for specialist support. You decide what to work on, what to share and when to pause or stop. You do not need to explain your personal history.

How we approach safety

We aim to work in an informal, trauma-informed way, with attention to safety, trust, choice and collaboration. Meetings are agreed in advance and can take place by Zoom, telephone or, by arrangement, through a community organisation or visit. You can tell us what communication or access adjustments would help.

Our safeguarding process

We have a process for raising and escalating safeguarding concerns. At the start of mentoring, we explain how to raise a concern, who we can turn to for safeguarding guidance and what happens if further help is needed.

Our first escalation route

We work closely with Waste Not Want Not Poole. The team there is trained in safeguarding, and we escalate safeguarding concerns to them for guidance and support with the next step.

An additional safeguarding route

A volunteer psychotherapist provides an additional route for safeguarding advice and escalation. They hold a degree in social policy and are trained in working with vulnerable people. They do not practise psychotherapy through Happyish; their role here is safeguarding support.

When further help is needed

These routes help us respond to concerns and identify appropriate further support. Where a concern needs a local authority safeguarding team, the police or emergency services, we escalate to the appropriate service. An immediate risk is not left waiting for a mentoring appointment.

Safeguarding training

Happyish’s founder will also undertake safeguarding training as part of preparing to deliver the service. This training complements the safeguarding support and escalation routes above.

If you have a concern

If you want to raise a concern about a mentoring interaction, contact Happyish at aha@happyish.org.uk or call 07731 384953. Please keep an initial message brief and do not include sensitive personal details unless they are needed. Our email and telephone are not monitored for emergency help.

If someone is in immediate danger in the UK, call 999. For non-urgent specialist help, we can discuss an appropriate service and support you to make contact. Information is not shared casually; if there is a serious safety concern, we may need to seek appropriate help.

Privacy and related support
