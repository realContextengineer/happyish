The initial model

Happyish is developing and piloting a one-to-one approach centred on real tasks, achievable goals and increasing independence. Delivery starts small so the model can learn from experience before expanding.

What we hope to measure

Potential measures include confidence with digital tools, ability to complete tasks independently, safer AI use, awareness of scams and privacy, and access to work, learning and digital public services.

These are proposed measures, not reported results. We do not yet publish measured impact or partner endorsements.

Future possibilities

Relaxed groups, AI-literacy workshops, community programmes and partnerships may follow. These are in development and are not established services.
