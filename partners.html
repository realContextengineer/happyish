What we do

✳

A little help.
At your pace.

Free, one-to-one mentoring that starts with what interests or matters to the person. We explore technology and AI together, making room for questions, experimentation and discovery. Confidence and capability can grow through those experiences, opening possibilities for learning, connection and work.

Informal, trauma-informed and led by the person’s own goals.
