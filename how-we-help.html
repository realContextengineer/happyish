Not sure how to use AI safely?

We can explore what AI can and cannot do, practise useful prompts and check outputs together. Learn about hallucinations, misinformation, bias, scams and over-reliance. Keep personal information out of prompts and recognise when a human professional is needed.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

An AI answer that sounds convincing, but you’re not sure whether it’s right. Or a tool you want to try without sharing private information.

What we’d do together

We can start with a low-risk task, write a prompt together and compare the answer with reliable sources. We’ll look at uncertainty, invented facts and what information to leave out. Then you can try a similar task yourself.

What you could take away

A way to ask useful questions and check the answer, rather than having to trust the tool.

Struggling with an online form?

We can work through the instructions, organise the information you need and help you understand each step. This can include accounts, public-service websites or health-related digital services. You stay in control of your information and decisions.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

An online application, an account you can’t access, or instructions that are difficult to follow. Bring the task; you don’t need to send us the personal details in advance.

What we’d do together

We can read the instructions, work out what information is needed and tackle one section at a time. You enter your own private details and make your own decisions. For specialist questions, we can help you identify the right support.

What you could take away

More confidence navigating the process and understanding what the next step asks of you.

Need help organising digital life?

Documents, email, online accounts, passwords, calendars and reminders: we can untangle the practical task and find a manageable routine. We can also explore accessibility settings and assistive tools.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

A crowded inbox, files you can never find, missed reminders or several accounts that have become difficult to manage.

What we’d do together

Choose one manageable problem first. We can practise a folder system, a calendar reminder or a simpler routine, and write down the steps in a format that works for you. Accessibility settings and assistive tools can be part of that.

What you could take away

A small routine you understand and can use again, rather than a complicated new system.

Trying to get back into work or learning?

We can work alongside you on the digital parts: finding opportunities, organising applications, preparing a CV or writing an email. The support starts with your own goals.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

An opportunity you’ve found, a CV document, a job-application website or an email you’re finding difficult to write.

What we’d do together

We can break the digital task into steps, organise the information and practise the tools together. If AI is useful, we can check its suggestions and keep your own experience and voice at the centre. You decide what to submit.

What you could take away

A clearer next action and more confidence using the same tools for another opportunity.

Worried about scams or privacy?

We can practise recognising suspicious messages, checking links and protecting accounts. We can help you understand online information without expecting you to become a security expert.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

A suspicious message, confusing privacy settings or uncertainty about whether a link or an AI-generated claim is trustworthy. Don’t forward passwords or financial details.

What we’d do together

We can explore warning signs, check the source through a separate trusted route and practise safer account habits. If money, identity or immediate safety is at risk, specialist help may be needed; we can help you work out the next step.

What you could take away

A repeatable pause-and-check approach, and a better understanding of when to seek specialist support.

Feel like technology is moving faster than you are?

Bring an unfamiliar app, a difficult letter, or a task you keep putting off. We can slow it down, break it into smaller steps and practise together. You don’t have to apologise for asking.

See an example & next steps+

What you might bring

An unfamiliar app, a letter you don’t understand, or one everyday digital task that feels harder than it should.

What we’d do together

We can slow it down, show one step, try it together and let you practise with support. Tell us if repetition, written instructions or a different pace would help. You don’t need a diagnosis or technical knowledge to begin.

What you could take away

A little more understanding, a practical next step and something you can try independently.
